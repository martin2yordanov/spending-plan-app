Privacy Policy

Spending Plan · Last updated 10 September 2026

Spending Plan is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains exactly what it stores, where that data goes, and how to get rid of it.

What is collected

DataWhyWhere it is stored
The financial plan you enter: income sources, expenses, categories and limits, savings account names and balances, recurring bills, investment amount, chosen currency It is the content of the app. Without it there is nothing to show you. On your device, and on our server so the same plan appears on your other devices
Your email address and account identifier To sign you in and to attach your plan to your account Held by our authentication provider, Clerk

No advertising identifiers. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No location, contacts, photos, health data or device identifiers. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with data brokers or advertisers.

Bank accounts

The app has no connection to any bank. It never asks for banking credentials, card numbers or account numbers, and it cannot read your transactions. Every figure in the app is one you typed in yourself.

Who processes your data

  • Clerk — authentication. Holds your email address and account identifier.
  • Vercel — hosting for the app and its API.
  • Redis Cloud — stores your saved plan.
  • Groq — only if you tap “Generate Suggestions”. Your plan figures are sent to a large language model to produce written advice. This is optional and happens only on that explicit tap; the app is fully usable without ever using it. The suggestions are generated text, not financial advice.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you switch on the optional app lock, the check is performed by iOS. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to this app — iOS only reports whether the check passed.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled locally on your device from the due dates you entered. No reminder data is sent anywhere, and no push notifications are sent from a server.

Deleting your data

Open the app, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. That permanently removes your stored plan and your account. It is immediate and cannot be undone — there is no backup to restore from. You can also delete the app to remove the copy held on your device.

Data retention

Your plan is kept for as long as your account exists, because its whole purpose is to still be there next time you open the app. Delete the account and it is removed.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
